Selling a House With a Broken HVAC System

Published: July 27, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A failed furnace or air-conditioning system can make a home uncomfortable and raise immediate concerns for buyers. It does not make the property impossible to sell, but the age of the equipment, repair cost, season, and preferred timeline should all shape the seller's plan.

Start With a Professional Diagnosis

Ask a qualified HVAC technician to identify the cause of the failure before assuming the entire system needs replacement. A written diagnosis can distinguish a smaller component repair from a larger problem involving the furnace, condenser, heat pump, ductwork, or electrical supply.

Compare Repair and Replacement Estimates

Get itemized estimates and ask what warranties apply. Consider the system's age, expected remaining life, energy efficiency, and whether other major components may soon need attention. The least expensive repair is not always the best investment before a sale.

Protect the Property While You Decide

Extreme heat or cold can create additional risks, including frozen pipes, excess humidity, or moisture damage. Use safe temporary measures when appropriate, monitor the home, and follow professional advice to prevent a broken HVAC system from causing more expensive problems.

Be Clear About the Known Condition

Keep service records and estimates available, and describe the known condition accurately. Disclosure requirements vary by location, so a local real estate professional or attorney can help explain which forms and details apply to the sale.

Consider Selling As-Is

An as-is cash sale may let a homeowner avoid paying for HVAC work before closing. Compare the offer and expected net proceeds with repair costs, buyer financing requirements, inspection negotiations, and the time needed to prepare the home for a traditional listing.
