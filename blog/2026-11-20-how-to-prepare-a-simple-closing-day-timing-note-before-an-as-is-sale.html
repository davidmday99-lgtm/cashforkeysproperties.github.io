How to Prepare a Simple Closing-Day Timing Note Before an As-Is Sale

Published: November 20, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A short timing note can make the last steps of an as-is sale feel easier to organize. Homeowners in Sarasota or St. Louis can use it to keep their personal plans and practical questions in one place before closing day.

Note the Times You Know

Start with the appointment or handover time you have been given, then add personal timing details such as moving help or travel. Leave space for questions instead of guessing about transaction details.

Keep Personal Plans Separate

A simple note can help you see how your own plans fit around the day. Keep it focused on your schedule and avoid including private account information or documents you do not need.

Confirm Details With the Right People

If a timing detail affects closing or access, ask your closing coordinator or title company contact. They can explain transaction-specific scheduling and tell you whether anything else is needed.

Use Official Instructions First

A timing note is a personal organizing tool, not legal, financial, or closing advice. When you receive transaction-specific instructions, rely on the title company and other qualified professionals involved in your sale.
