How to Organize a Simple Key and Access Plan Before an As-Is Sale

Published: October 3, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A simple key and access plan can make the final steps of an as-is home sale feel far less hectic. Whether your property is in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, taking a few minutes to account for the ways people enter the home can help the handover stay clear.

Gather Every Type of Key

Start by collecting front-door keys, back-door keys, garage keys, mailbox keys, gate keys, and any spare sets you know about. Place them in one labeled envelope or small bag rather than leaving them in several drawers, vehicles, or lockboxes.

Include Remotes and Access Devices

Set aside garage-door remotes, gate remotes, fobs, and parking passes with the keys. If a device has more than one button, a quick note about what it controls can be useful. You do not need to solve every small detail—just keep related items together.

Make a Short Access List

Write down the locations that each item opens, especially if a key is not obvious. For example, note whether a key is for a storage room, a side gate, or a community mailbox. This helps prevent the last-minute question of which key belongs where.

Check for Shared or Hidden Keys

Think about any keys held by a relative, neighbor, tenant, or service provider. If a spare has been hidden outside, retrieve it when appropriate. If someone else needs to return a key, give them clear timing and a simple place to leave it.

Follow the Agreed Handover Instructions

Use the instructions already provided by the title contact, buyer's representative, or other transaction contact for the actual handover. If the plan changes, communicate the change early instead of making assumptions about where keys should go.

Keep the Plan Easy to Find

Put the key bundle and your short access list with the items you will take to the final appointment. A small amount of organization lets you focus on moving forward, without adding unnecessary work to an as-is sale.
