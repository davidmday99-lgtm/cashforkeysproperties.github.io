How to Make a Simple Final Handover Summary Before an As-Is Sale

Published: October 23, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A brief final handover summary can make the last steps of an as-is home sale feel more organized. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, it is a simple way to keep the most useful details together without creating a large project.

Keep It Short and Practical

Write down the information that may be helpful at handover, such as a timing note, key count, access detail, or a reminder about remaining belongings. A few clear lines are usually more useful than a long document.

Start With the Final Schedule

Include the date and time that matter most to you, along with any questions you still have. Seeing the schedule first can make it easier to decide what needs attention before the final day.

List Access Details Together

Note keys, remotes, mailbox keys, gate codes, or other access items in one place. A short list can help you gather those smaller items before they become a last-minute search.

Separate Home Notes From Personal Records

Keep any useful property notes in the summary, but take personal papers, valuables, medications, and financial records with you. This makes it easier to keep the handover information focused and your important items close.

Save Questions for One Conversation

Add a small section for questions about timing, access, or what remains at the property. Keeping them together gives you a calm, clear starting point when you are ready to talk through the next step.

Review It Once Before Handover

Look over the summary shortly before the final day and update anything that changed. It does not need to cover every detail; it simply needs to make the handover feel clear and manageable.
