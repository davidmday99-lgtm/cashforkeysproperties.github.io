How to Make a Move-Out Checklist for an As-Is Sale

Published: September 25, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A move-out checklist can make the final days before an as-is sale feel more manageable. For a Sarasota-area or St. Louis home, the goal is not to create a perfect property; it is to keep your belongings, access details, and next steps organized around the timeline you have agreed on.

Start With the Date That Matters Most

Write down the planned closing or handover date, then work backward. Note deadlines for moving trucks, donation pickups, utility changes, and any property visit that has already been arranged. Keeping these items in one place makes it easier to see what needs attention first.

Sort Belongings One Area at a Time

Choose a room, closet, or storage area and decide what you will keep, donate, recycle, or discard. Label boxes as you go and set aside personal documents, medications, jewelry, and small valuables so they do not get mixed into a donation or removal pile.

Focus on Access, Not Unnecessary Repairs

An as-is sale does not require you to take on cosmetic projects just to prepare the home. Instead, make sure agreed access areas are clear and note anything a visitor should know about a gate, pet, parking, or a room that cannot yet be entered. Share only the details relevant to the appointment.

Plan Utilities and Mail Carefully

Confirm when service should remain on and when it can be transferred or stopped. Forward mail to your new address, update important accounts, and keep a list of confirmation numbers. If you have questions about timing, ask the provider or your transaction contact before making a change.

Save a Final-Day Essentials Bag

Keep keys, chargers, identification, important paperwork, and a change of clothes in a bag that stays with you. A short checklist for the final day can include checking cabinets, locking windows, collecting remotes, and taking any personal items that were set aside.

Review the Plan Before You Leave

Look over your checklist once more and mark off completed items. If an unexpected issue affects the agreed schedule, communicate it promptly rather than guessing at a solution. A simple plan gives you room to handle the final details without turning them into a larger project.
