How to Keep a Simple Closing-Day Contact List Before an As-Is Sale

Published: November 17, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A simple contact list can make the last steps of an as-is sale feel easier to manage. Homeowners in Sarasota or St. Louis can use it to keep the right names and numbers nearby without mixing personal notes into official transaction records.

List the Essential Contacts

Start with the people you may need to reach, such as the title company contact, your closing coordinator, and anyone helping with a planned move. Add only the details you need to identify how and when to contact them.

Note Each Person’s Role

A short label beside each name can prevent confusion when questions come up. For example, mark who handles closing logistics and who can answer a question about access or timing.

Keep the List Easy to Find

Save the list somewhere you can reach on closing day, whether that is a note on your phone or a single page with your personal documents. Avoid adding private account details that do not need to be shared.

Use Official Instructions First

A contact list is a personal organizing tool, not legal, financial, or closing advice. When you receive transaction-specific instructions, rely on the title company and other qualified professionals involved in your sale.
