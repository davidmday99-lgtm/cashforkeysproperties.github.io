How to Make a Simple Closing-Day Reminders Note Before an As-Is Sale

Published: November 26, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A short reminders note can help make the last steps of an as-is sale feel more manageable. Homeowners in Sarasota or St. Louis can use it to keep personal tasks and practical questions in one place before closing day.

Start With Personal Tasks

Write down the personal tasks you want to complete before leaving, such as checking a closet, gathering everyday items, or confirming a ride. A brief list can keep those details from competing for attention.

Keep Questions Visible

If you have a question for the title company or another professional involved in the sale, add it to the note with a few details. This makes it easier to ask the right person when the time comes.

Leave Room for Changes

Closing-day plans can change, so keep the note flexible. Cross off finished items and add a short update when something needs confirmation.

Follow Official Instructions

A reminders note is a personal organizing tool, not legal, financial, or closing advice. Follow instructions from the title company and other qualified professionals involved in your sale when they apply to your transaction.
