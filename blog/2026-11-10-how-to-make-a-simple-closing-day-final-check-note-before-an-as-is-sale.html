How to Make a Simple Closing-Day Final Check Note Before an As-Is Sale

Published: November 10, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A short final check note can make an as-is closing day feel easier to manage. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, it is a personal way to gather a few reminders before leaving the property while the title company and other professionals handle the official closing work.

Use Confirmed Details First

Begin with the timing and steps you have already confirmed. Keep your note separate from formal closing instructions, and rely on the appropriate professionals if any official detail needs clarification or changes.

Walk Through Personal Spaces

Add a reminder to check the places where personal items can be easy to overlook, such as closets, cabinets, storage areas, and outdoor spaces. A simple list can make the final walk-through less dependent on memory.

Keep Access Items in View

If you have received instructions about keys, remotes, garage openers, or other access items, include a brief prompt on your note. The people coordinating the closing can confirm what is needed and where those items should be left.

Write Down Open Questions

Leave room for a question or a last-minute update. Capturing it in one place can help you contact the right professional instead of sorting through several messages during a busy day.

Make It a Personal Tool

A final check note is a personal organizing aid, not legal, financial, or closing advice. Reviewing it before you go can help you feel prepared and gives you a clear chance to ask an appropriate professional about anything that is uncertain.
