How to Make a Simple Final Handover Plan Before an As-Is Sale

Published: October 18, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A simple final handover plan can make the last part of an as-is home sale feel more manageable. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, a few clear notes can help keep timing, access, and personal tasks from becoming a last-minute scramble.

Confirm the Main Details

Start with the handover date, the expected time, and the details you have already confirmed. Put them in one place so you have a simple reference when you are planning the final days.

Choose a Short Task List

List only the tasks that matter most, such as gathering keys, separating personal belongings, or making sure access items are ready. A short list is easier to use than a long plan that creates more pressure.

Keep Access Items Together

Decide where keys, remotes, garage openers, and other access devices will be kept until the handover. If an item needs a note or instruction, add that detail to the plan as well.

Leave Room for Questions

Write down any remaining questions about timing, belongings, or property access. Seeing the questions together can make it easier to get the information you need before the final day.

Prepare What You Will Take

Set aside your personal documents, medications, valuables, and essentials separately. This gives you a clear line between the items that stay with you and the practical handover details.

Review the Plan Once More

Look over the plan the day before the handover. The goal is not perfection—it is simply to make sure the important details are clear and that you know what still needs attention.
