How to Prepare a Simple Property Handover Folder

Published: September 24, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A small property handover folder can keep practical details together as you prepare for an as-is sale. For a Sarasota-area or St. Louis home, it does not need to be complicated: a labeled folder or secure digital folder can hold the notes and records that may be useful during the final steps.

Start With a One-Page Property Summary

Write down the property address, the best contact number, and any agreed appointment dates. Add short notes about access, parking, or a gate code only when those details need to be shared. Keep sensitive information out of the folder unless a qualified professional specifically asks for it.

Gather Useful Equipment and Service Notes

Include appliance manuals, warranty information, and simple operating notes for items that will stay with the property. A note about a thermostat, sprinkler timer, or garage remote can be helpful. Keep it factual and brief rather than trying to create a complete repair history.

Separate Personal and Transaction Records

Personal financial papers, IDs, and private correspondence should stay with you. Keep sale-related records in a separate place from the handover folder unless your transaction professional needs them. Review every page before leaving it behind.

Keep a Current Key and Remote List

List the keys, remotes, fobs, and access devices you plan to hand over. Note where each item is stored and mark it off when delivered. This list can help you avoid a last-minute search for a mailbox key or garage remote.

Use Copies When You Need to Keep the Original

If a manual or receipt is important for your own records, keep the original and provide a copy if appropriate. Digital photos or scans can make it easier to retain your documentation. Check with the relevant provider or professional before sharing documents that include private account details.

Review the Folder Near Handover

Remove outdated notes and confirm that the contents match the current plan. Tell your transaction contact about anything unusual that could affect access or a scheduled visit. A short, organized folder can make the final handover more straightforward for everyone involved.
