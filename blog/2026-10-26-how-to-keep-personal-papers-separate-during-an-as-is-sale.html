How to Keep Personal Papers Separate During an As-Is Sale

Published: October 26, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

Packing can mix home information with records you want to keep private. A simple sorting routine helps Sarasota-area and St. Louis homeowners keep those papers under control while preparing for an as-is sale.

Start With Two Folders

Use one folder for your personal records and another for useful property information. Keep identification, bank statements, medical records, and account details in your personal folder.

Check the Places Paper Collects

Look through desk drawers, kitchen organizers, filing cabinets, and boxes in closets. Work through one small area at a time so the task stays manageable.

Review Manuals Before Sharing

Appliance instructions and equipment notes may be useful to the next owner. Check them for names, account numbers, or passwords before deciding what to share.

Keep a Copy of Your Sale Records

Store your own sale documents where you can find them after moving. Choose a folder or secure digital location you already use, and avoid putting private records in a shared property folder.

Carry the Personal Folder Yourself

Keep sensitive papers with you during the move. Before handover, check that no personal mail or files remain in drawers or storage areas.
