How to Take Property Photos for an As-Is Sale

Published: September 8, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

You do not need professional listing photos to start a conversation about selling your house as-is. A clear, honest set of pictures gives a buyer a useful starting point and helps you explain the property without preparing it for a full round of showings.

Start With a Simple Walkthrough

Photograph the front and back of the house, then take an overview of each room from the doorway or another accessible spot. Include the kitchen, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms, and any garage or outdoor space. Keep the camera level and use natural light where possible so the rooms are easy to understand.

Show the Repairs You Already Know About

Include visible concerns such as damaged flooring, ceiling stains, worn cabinets, or broken fixtures. Take one wider picture for context and one closer picture for detail. Describe what you have observed without guessing at the cause, and avoid filters or edits that hide the property's condition.

Keep the Process Manageable

You do not have to empty every room or repaint the house just to share initial photos. Move small items only when doing so is easy and helps show a feature. If belongings block a room or an area is inaccessible, mention that in your notes instead of trying to make the photo set look complete.

Protect Your Privacy and Stay on Safe Ground

Check each image for mail, account statements, family photos, keys, and security codes before sending it. Avoid including other people without their permission. Take pictures only from safely accessible areas; a roof, crawl space, or damaged floor does not need a risky close-up.

Add Short Notes Before Sharing

Label the pictures by room and note anything important that the camera misses, such as an area you could not access or a repair you have already completed. Ask the buyer how to send the files. Photos can support an initial discussion, but a buyer may still need a walkthrough or further information before confirming an offer.
