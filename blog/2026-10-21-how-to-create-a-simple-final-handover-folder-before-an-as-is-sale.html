How to Create a Simple Final Handover Folder Before an As-Is Sale

Published: October 21, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A simple final handover folder can make the last steps of an as-is home sale feel more organized. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, it is an easy way to keep useful details together without creating a major project.

Choose One Easy Place

Use a folder, envelope, or clearly labeled document sleeve for the information you want to keep handy. The goal is simply to have one dependable spot instead of several loose notes around the house.

Add Basic Property Details

Include simple items that may be helpful at handover, such as spare key labels, garage remote notes, mailbox information, or a short list of access details. Keep the notes clear and only include what is relevant.

Keep Personal Papers Separate

Important personal documents, medications, valuables, and financial records should travel with you rather than stay in the property folder. Separating them early can make packing and the final walk-through less stressful.

Make a Short Utility Note

If there are utility or service details you need to remember, write down the account steps or timing you plan to follow. A short reminder can help you avoid relying on memory during a busy move.

Include Your Remaining Questions

Keep any last questions about timing, belongings, or access in the same folder. That gives you one place to review before you speak with the people involved in the sale.

Review It Before Handover

Take a few minutes to look through the folder before the final day. It does not need to be perfect; it only needs to make the important details easier to find and manage.
