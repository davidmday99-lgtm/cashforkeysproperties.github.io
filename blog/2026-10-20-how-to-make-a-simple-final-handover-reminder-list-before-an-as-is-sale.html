How to Make a Simple Final Handover Reminder List Before an As-Is Sale

Published: October 20, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A simple final handover reminder list can make the last days of an as-is home sale feel less overwhelming. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, a short list can keep the important details visible without turning the process into a bigger project.

Start With the Items You Will Hand Over

Write down the items that need to be available at handover, such as house keys, mailbox keys, garage remotes, and gate or alarm instructions. Keeping them in one place makes the final step easier to manage.

Keep Personal Essentials Separate

Set aside documents, medications, valuables, and everyday essentials that should travel with you. A reminder list can help you check that these personal items are not left behind or packed with things staying at the property.

Note Any Access Details

If there are codes, labels, or simple instructions that will help with access, add them to your list. You do not need a long explanation; a clear note is often enough to keep the handover organized.

Confirm Your Final Timing

Add the date and time you expect to finish packing, meet for the handover, or make your last trip to the property. Seeing those points together can help you pace the remaining tasks.

Keep Questions in One Place

If you have remaining questions about belongings, utilities, or access, put them on the same list. This gives you a simple place to review before the final day instead of relying on memory.

Check the List Once More

Take a few minutes to review the list before you leave. The goal is not perfection; it is simply to make the important handover details clear and manageable.
