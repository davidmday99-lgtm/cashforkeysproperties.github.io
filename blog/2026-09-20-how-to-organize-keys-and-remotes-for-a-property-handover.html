How to Organize Keys and Remotes for a Property Handover

Published: September 20, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

House keys are easy to remember, but a property may also have a mailbox key, garage remote, gate control, or shed padlock. For homeowners preparing an as-is sale in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, a small access inventory can make the handover easier to coordinate. Start gathering the details early and confirm the transfer arrangements with your transaction contact.

Make a List of Access Points

Walk through the property and list the doors, gates, garages, mailboxes, and storage areas that use a separate key or device. Note anything you cannot locate or have not used recently. Share those gaps ahead of time so the handover conversation includes the full picture.

Label Items Without Adding an Address

Use simple labels such as front door, side gate, or garage remote. Keep the property address and other identifying details in your private checklist instead of attaching them to a loose key ring. Store the labeled items together in a secure place until the agreed transfer.

Check Which Items Work

When you have permission to access the property, match each key or remote to the entry it operates. Record a sticky lock, missing battery, or device that does not respond. Describe what you observed rather than promising that every item is ready to use, and avoid testing anything that would disturb an occupant or create an access problem.

Account for Copies Held by Others

Note whether a family member, property manager, neighbor, or service provider has an extra key or remote. Coordinate with the appropriate person about collecting those items. If the home is occupied, include the property contact in the plan and keep access arrangements consistent with the agreed process.

Keep Digital Access Details Private

List smart locks, gate systems, or connected garage controls that may need separate handover instructions. Keep codes and account details out of public listing notes and ordinary key labels. Ask how the device should be transferred, and use the manufacturer's instructions for any account changes at the agreed time.

Confirm the Transfer and Keep a Record

Agree on who will receive the access items, where the exchange will happen, and when it is scheduled. Bring your inventory so both sides can identify what is included and what is still missing. Keep a copy of the list with your other sale notes for any follow-up questions.
