How to Keep a Simple Key and Access Note for Handover

Published: October 29, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A short key and access note can make the final handover easier to follow without turning it into a complicated project. For homeowners in the Sarasota area or St. Louis, the goal is simply to record the practical details that have already been agreed.

List Only the Items Being Handed Over

Start with the keys, remotes, and access cards that belong with the property. A plain list such as front door key, mailbox key, or garage remote gives everyone an easy reference point.

Use Clear Locations

Note where each item will be left or who will receive it. If a key will be provided at closing, say that instead of guessing about a time or place that has not been confirmed.

Keep Private Codes Separate

Do not put alarm codes, passwords, account numbers, or other private information on a general handover note. Share sensitive access details only through the private method agreed with the appropriate recipient.

Flag Missing or Uncertain Items

If an older key no longer works or a remote is missing, write that down plainly. A clear note is more useful than trying to replace an item or make a promise you cannot verify.

Review the Note Before the Final Step

Take a quick look at the list with the person handling the sale and update it if the plan changes. Keeping the note short and factual helps everyone stay on the same page.
