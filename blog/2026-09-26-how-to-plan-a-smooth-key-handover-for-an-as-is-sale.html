How to Plan a Smooth Key Handover for an As-Is Sale

Published: September 26, 2026By: Cash For Keys Properties

A key handover is a small but important milestone in an as-is sale. For a Sarasota-area or St. Louis home, a simple plan can keep the final exchange clear without adding last-minute pressure or unnecessary work to the property.

Confirm the Handover Timing

Start with the agreed closing or possession date and ask when keys should be delivered. Write down the time, location, and person handling the exchange. If a title company, attorney, or other contact is involved, use their instructions rather than making assumptions about timing.

Gather Every Access Item

Collect house keys, mailbox keys, garage remotes, gate fobs, pool keys, and any other access devices you plan to provide. Put them in one labeled envelope or small container so nothing is left in a drawer, vehicle, or key hook on the final day.

Leave Useful Property Notes

A short note can be helpful when it covers practical details such as the trash day, alarm instructions, or the location of a shutoff valve. Keep the information straightforward and avoid promising repairs, timelines, or services that have not been agreed upon.

Make a Final Personal Belongings Check

Before you leave, look through closets, cabinets, the garage, storage areas, and outdoor spaces for personal items. Keep documents, medications, jewelry, chargers, and valuables with you. A room-by-room check helps prevent a forgotten item from becoming a rushed return trip.

Keep Utilities and Access Aligned

Follow the plan for utilities, mail, and any scheduled service visits. If power or water needs to remain active through a certain date, confirm that with the provider. Let the appropriate transaction contact know promptly if an access issue or schedule change comes up.

Document the Completed Handover

Once the keys are delivered, note when and where it happened and keep any receipt or confirmation with your sale paperwork. A simple record gives you a clear reference point as you finish the rest of your move and close out property-related accounts.
